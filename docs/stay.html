Where to base yourself

Four neighbourhoods, each with its own feel. Distances are straight-line from Jnane Rumi — the drive is a little longer.

Getting here

Marrakech Menara Airport · RAK

The airport is on the south-west edge of the city. Expect roughly 25–35 minutes by car to the Palmeraie, depending on traffic.

There are no wedding shuttles — taxis and ride-hailing apps run to the venue.
