Good to know

Weather

Early May is warm and sunny in Marrakech, with cooler evenings. Bring a light layer for Saturday night, as the party runs until 4 am.

Money

The currency is the Moroccan dirham (MAD). Cards work in hotels and restaurants; carry some cash for taxis and the souks.

Plugs

Type C and E (European two-pin), 220 V.

Getting around

Taxis are easy to find. Agree the fare before you set off, or ask for the meter.
